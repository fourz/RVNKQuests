# ~TALES FROM A HAT~

There is a black skeleton standing in the overworld.

Not in a fortress. Not underground. Out in the open, in daylight, where nothing like it belongs.

---

# WHERE

The far portals, at the edge of the world. Nobody goes out there now.

You did once. That is where the sun went out last January.

---

# THE CAMP

Somebody set up beside the portals. Bedroll, spare tools, a fire gone cold.

They never packed it away. I do not know if they ran or simply stopped.

---

# IT WILL FIGHT YOU

It is exactly as hostile as it looks, and its blade carries the withering.

Do not trade hits with it. Do not let it back you toward the drop.

---

# IT GUARDS THE SKY

It did not wander out there. It was posted, and it has held the post.

Ask what is up there worth guarding, and why nobody has looked since the sun came back.

---

# GO AND SEE

It is carrying something, and has been the whole time.

Put it down and take the book off him.

Bring a sword, and milk.

*~ The Hat*
