# ~TALES FROM A HAT~

You came through. Good.

Most people stand at the gap a long while first, working out whether it is a door or a hole.

---

# WHERE YOU STAND

This is still the same sky. The islands ahead are the ones Zeal took with them.

You will not need to leave them again until you are done.

---

# WHICH WAY

North.

Two hundred and fifty paces of it, near enough. Let yourself drift a little east as you go.

Downward, too. Not far. Five blocks lower than where your boots are now.

# YOU WILL KNOW IT

A chapel, or what the centuries left of one.

Open roof. Broken pillars. Stone that was cut carefully by people who expected to come back.

---

# THE LECTERN

Inside it, still standing, there is a lectern.

They left it facing the door. That was deliberate. They wanted whoever came after to read the thing on their way in, not on their way out.

Put your hand on it.

# LAST

Zeal will have told you one true thing at last.

*~ The Hat*
